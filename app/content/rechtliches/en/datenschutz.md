This text describes which data Fourwalls actually collects when you use this website, and what it is used for. It is a draft and does not replace a legal review — the binding version will follow before the application goes live publicly.

## Controller

The controller is Fourwalls. Full contact details are given in the legal notice; some of them are not yet confirmed.

## What data we collect

**Property enquiries.** When you request a viewing or information about a listing, we collect your name, email address, optionally a phone number, and your message. This information is passed to the party offering the property to handle your enquiry, and is at the same time stored by Fourwalls.

**Enquiries to Fourwalls.** When you submit an enquiry about selling, letting, valuation, management, or consultation, we collect your name, email address, optionally a phone number, an optional preferred appointment, and — depending on the enquiry — details about the property concerned (location, property type, floor area, year built, condition, and similar). These enquiries go exclusively to Fourwalls.

**Account.** For an account, we collect name, email address, and a password. Saved properties, search alerts, your own listings, and enquiries are linked to the account once one exists.

**Listings.** Anyone who publishes their own listing enters property details, images, and contact information, which become visible once published.

## Purpose of processing

We use this information to route and handle enquiries, to run an account, and to publish listings. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not pass it on to third parties for advertising purposes.

## IP addresses

For enquiries, the IP address is not stored in plain text. A salted, non-reversible hash value is stored instead, used exclusively to detect and prevent abuse.

## Session cookie

A sign-in is kept using a cookie named `fw.session_token`. It is strictly technically necessary (HttpOnly, SameSite Lax), so it cannot be read by scripts in the browser, and is valid for seven days by default; it is extended while you remain active. It serves no purpose other than the sign-in itself.

## Local storage in the browser

Without an account, the browser remembers a few things only on your own device, not at Fourwalls:

- Saved properties list (`fw-merkliste`)
- Comparison list (`fw-vergleich`)
- Recently viewed properties (`fw-verlauf`)
- Light or dark display mode (`fw-ufer`)
- Draft of an enquiry in progress (`fw-anliegen-…`)
- Draft of a listing started before signing in (`fw-entwurf-vorab`)

None of this leaves your device unless you sign in; afterwards, the saved list and the draft can be merged into the account.

## No tracking, no advertising

This website uses no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party advertising integration.

## External services

Map tiles are sourced from swisstopo (vectortiles.geo.admin.ch); if that service does not respond, OpenFreeMap serves as a fallback. Fonts and the map library are served by Fourwalls itself, not loaded from a third-party provider.

## Retention periods

Retention periods have not yet been defined and will be added before the public launch.
