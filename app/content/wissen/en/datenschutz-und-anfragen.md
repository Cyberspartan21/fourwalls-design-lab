This page explains in plain terms what Fourwalls does with your information. It does not replace the privacy policy, which is currently undergoing legal review, but describes the website's actual behaviour.

## When you enquire about a property

Your message, your name and your contact details go to the party offering the property and to Fourwalls for processing. For properties that Fourwalls markets itself, Fourwalls is the recipient. They are not passed on to third parties, such as advertising partners. You can see your enquiries in your account, if you have one.

## When you send a request

Requests relating to selling, letting, valuation, management or advice go exclusively to Fourwalls, never to estate agencies or other providers on the marketplace. You receive a confirmation by email with a reference number. Fourwalls stores the information you provide in the form, and the time you submitted it. The IP address is not stored, only a non-reversible value to prevent abuse.

## When you open an account

An account needs an email address and a password. Sign-in is kept with a cookie that serves only the session and expires after seven days of inactivity. Saved listings, search alerts, your own listings and your requests are linked to the account.

## Without an account

You can search for and discover properties without an account. Saved listings, comparison and recently viewed properties are then stored only on your device, in the browser. Fourwalls does not see these lists.

## No advertising, no tracking

The website does not use analytics or advertising services and does not embed content from social networks. Without signing in, it sets no cookies. The map loads map imagery from swisstopo, the Federal Office of Topography; swisstopo then sees the technical request for the map tiles requested. Fonts and the map library are served by Fourwalls itself.

## What is still open

How long requests and enquiries are retained has not yet been determined and will be added before the public launch. Until then, Fourwalls deletes nothing automatically. If you would like data to be deleted, write to the contact address given in the legal notice.

You can find the full [privacy policy](/en/datenschutz) and the [legal notice](/en/impressum) in the footer of every page.
