Letting means entering into a relationship that can last for years. Care at the start determines peace of mind afterwards. This page describes the usual process in Switzerland and the points where Fourwalls can help.

## 1. Condition and rent

Check the property before letting it: what will be put in order, what stays as it is? The rent is guided by location, size, condition and comparable listings. Some cantons require a form for a new letting that discloses the previous rent. Fourwalls does not state figures here; the appropriate rent is an assessment specific to the property.

## 2. Listing

A good rental listing states floor area, rooms, floor, service charges, availability, pets, parking and viewing arrangements. Daylight photos and a floor plan reduce the number of unnecessary viewings. You can [create the listing yourself](/en/inserieren) or [ask Fourwalls to find tenants](/en/vermieten).

## 3. Reviewing applications

Ask only for what is needed for the decision: application form, debt collection register extract, ID, proof of income, references. Handle the documents confidentially and destroy them after the decision if no letting comes about.

## 4. Tenancy agreement

The contract sets out rent, service charges, deposit, start date, notice periods and house rules. The deposit for residential premises is capped at three months' rent and must be paid into a blocked account in the tenant's name. Terminations by the landlord must be given on the canton's official form, otherwise they are void.

## 5. Handover

A careful handover record with photos protects both sides. Note meter readings, keys and existing defects, and have the record signed by both parties.

## First letting

For new buildings and after major renovations, comparable values and experience with the property are lacking. Fourwalls offers first letting as a service: listing, applications, contracts and handover from a single source. The scope is agreed in conversation.

## If management should continue

Anyone who does not want to handle rent, service charges and maintenance themselves after letting can [request management](/en/verwalten). What management covers is explained on the page [Property management: services explained simply](/en/wissen/immobilienverwaltung-leistungen).
