Many websites show a franc amount after three fields are filled in. Fourwalls does not do that. This page explains why, and what you can expect instead.

## Three terms, three meanings

A **valuation** is a reasoned opinion on the value of a property. It draws on location, condition, floor area, year of construction and comparable listings. It is a basis for decisions, not proof.

A **hedonic valuation** is a statistical estimate based on comparative data. Banks use it when financing standard properties. It is only as good as the data behind it and does not know the individual house.

An **appraisal** is a written, reasoned determination of value by a qualified professional, for example for inheritance settlements, divorces or court proceedings. It has a defined purpose and a reference date.

## Why no automatic figure

A figure on screen looks precise, even when it is not. Two flats with identical basic data can differ considerably in value, because of the view, noise, the state of the kitchen or the condominium association's rules. A figure without conversation conceals these differences. Fourwalls would rather explain than impress.

## What a good valuation needs

- The address, or at least the municipality and neighbourhood.
- Type of property, living area, number of rooms, plot size for houses.
- Year of construction and any major renovations, with the year.
- Current use: owner-occupied, let, vacant.
- Your time horizon, if you are considering a sale.
