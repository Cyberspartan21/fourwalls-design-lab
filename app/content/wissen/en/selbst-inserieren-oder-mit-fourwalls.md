Fourwalls offers two deliberately different paths. Both lead to the same marketplace, but the work is shared differently.

## List it yourself

You create the listing, upload photos, answer enquiries and conduct viewings. Fourwalls checks the listing for completeness and compliance with marketplace rules before publishing it. A standard listing has no listing fee.

This path suits you if you have time, know the property well and want to handle contact with interested parties yourself. You keep the text, photos and enquiries in your own hands.

What you should bring: compelling photos, complete facts, availability for follow-up questions, and willingness to organise viewings.

## Sell with Fourwalls

You hand over marketing. After an enquiry, a personal conversation follows, in which the property, situation and approach are discussed. Fourwalls then prepares text and photos, markets the property on the marketplace, accompanies viewings and supports the negotiation through to completion.

This path suits you if you have little time, are not on site, or prefer to conduct negotiations with support. Fees and mandate terms are agreed in the personal conversation; Fourwalls deliberately does not quote flat figures here, because they depend on the property.

## The difference in one sentence

When listing it yourself, Fourwalls is the marketplace. When selling with Fourwalls, Fourwalls is additionally the party working for you.

## What both paths have in common

The order of search results is the same in both cases and cannot be bought. Properties that Fourwalls markets itself are labelled Fourwalls Exclusive, so interested parties can see who they are dealing with.

Anyone who is undecided can start with a [no-obligation valuation](/en/bewertung). It does not commit you to either path.
